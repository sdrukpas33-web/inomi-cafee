INOMI started as a club project at Gedu College of Business — a handful of students who wanted a place on campus that wasn't the canteen and wasn't your room.

No espresso machine, no pretense. Just fruit, tea, milk, and names that make you smile before the first sip.

The idea

A calm room with a silly menu.

We kept the space simple — warm wood tones, low noise, room to sit with a friend or a textbook. Then we let the drink names do all the talking. Order a "Water You Melon?" with a straight face. We won't judge. Much.
